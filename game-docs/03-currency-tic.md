# Chronoseria: Universal Currency System

Game design document · Economy reference for the entire series.

## 1. Currency Identity

- **Currency Name:** Time Investment Credits
- **Abbreviation:** TIC
- **Number System:** Base-ten metric system
- **Scope:** The entire Chronoseria video game series

TIC is the primary currency used to purchase items, weapons, armor, accommodation, transportation, and other goods and services throughout the Chronoseria series.

Its name reflects the idea that time is a valuable resource that can be spent, invested, and used to obtain future benefits.

## 2. Currency Format

The standard display format is the numerical amount followed by TIC.

Examples:

- 10 TIC
- 100 TIC
- 1,000 TIC
- 10,000 TIC

## 3. Uses of TIC

TIC can be spent on:

1. Items and consumable supplies.
2. Weapons and armor.
3. Inns and accommodation.
4. Transportation and travel services.
5. Skill learning and specialized training.
6. Equipment repairs and upgrades.
7. Other goods and services introduced in future games.

## 4. Series-Wide Rules

- TIC remains the primary currency across every Chronoseria game.
- Prices may vary according to location, item rarity, and economic conditions.
- Special currencies may exist for specific quests or activities, but they do not replace TIC as the primary currency.
- The currency's purchasing power should remain reasonably consistent across the series unless the story establishes an economic change.

## 5. Currency Lore

TIC represents the value of time, effort, and resources invested to obtain something.

Example NPC dialogue:

“I wouldn't spend those TICs lightly. They are the best investment your future self will ever make.”
